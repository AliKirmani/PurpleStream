Privacy & practical limits.

Your account stays local

There is no bundled account. Remembered settings, favorites and viewing history are encrypted for the current Windows user using DPAPI, in the local PurpleStream settings folder. This does not secure plain HTTP provider traffic. Your provider receives account/catalog/stream requests. Public flag requests contain country codes, not provider credentials. No application analytics or cloud sync is implemented.

Keep private files private

Downloads, recordings and snapshots are ordinary local files. Never share personal playlists, saved settings, credentials or unredacted account screenshots in issues. Review diagnostics before uploading.

What depends on your source?

EPG needs schedules; catch-up needs archives. Seeking, tracks, formats and downloads depend on stream support. Multiscreen and recording consume provider connections. There is no DRM bypass, scheduled recording or universal provider compatibility. Local synthetic tests do not guarantee every subscription or codec will work.
