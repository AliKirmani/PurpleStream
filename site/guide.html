GET STARTED

Your viewing space, in minutes.

Install and connect

Download the setup EXE from the repository's Releases section, install and open Purple Stream from Start. For portable use, extract the entire ZIP and keep all files together. Open Account and select Xtream Codes or M3U. Enter your own provider details or choose a local M3U file.

Find and play

Choose Live TV, Movies or Series. Browse country and genre, use search, and save favorites. In Multiscreen choose 2/3/4 screens, Equal or Main + sub screens, and Choose / Play for each slot. Each Audio button controls its own screen. Hidden screens stop.

Record and download

Download saves supported movies/episodes. Record captures a live stream until stopped or its timer ends. Preview stops by default to free a provider connection; watching while recording requires an extra allowed connection. Wait for finalization; .part files are incomplete.

Something unavailable?

EPG and catch-up need provider schedules/archives. Check your subscription and connection quota. Live streams may not seek. Share only redacted error information in issues; never post credentials or full playlist/stream URLs.
